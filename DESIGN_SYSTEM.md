# Rally 8.0 MVP Design System

## Visual direction
Playful premium: warm, colorful, encouraging, and grown-up.

## Palette
- Deep ink: #25243A
- Rally purple: #6257DF
- Lavender: #A89FF8
- Coral: #F58B76
- Rose: #E983AD
- Teal: #47B6A6
- Gold: #E5B64A
- Success green: #52A976
- Warm app background: #F8F7FB

## Design rules
- White cards carry most content.
- Hero gradients are reserved for major page identity.
- Color indicates meaning rather than decorating every surface.
- Large numbers receive visual priority.
- Status states use soft background tints rather than alarming colors.
- Cards use a shared radius/shadow system.
- Forms and settings use consistent spacing and field heights.
- No feature/functionality was intentionally removed from Phase 7.6.

## Functional baseline
Rally 8.0 is visually layered on top of Phase 7.6 and retains:
Rally Spaces, All My Rally, Profile control center, activities, undo, approvals,
settings/lifecycle controls, goals, treats, leaderboard, stats, community,
friends/comments, notifications, account preferences, capacity views, and admin tools.
